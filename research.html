Research

Four themes in the mathematical modelling of plant diseases

Four themes structure the research programme. All start from one question: how much of a disease’s dynamics comes from the plant, the pathogen, the vector or the way the crop is managed?

Spatial dynamics, barrier and dilution effects

Spatial SEIR models, reaction-diffusion systems, the basic reproduction number R0 and bifurcations, applied for example to wheat yellow rust.

Vectors, host preference and vegetative propagation

The role of vector host preference and vegetative multiplication in the emergence and persistence of diseases.

Optimal control and crop management

Cost-effective control strategies: clean seed use, harvesting and sanitation, multiseason models (coffee berry borer).

Soil-borne pathogens, multiple strains and quantitative resistance

Coexistence of strains in the soil, evolution of a fungus’s aggressiveness and the effect of partial plant resistance.
