About

Background and areas of expertise

I am a Lecturer in Applied Mathematics at the University of Dschang working in the field of mathematical modeling in life sciences. More specifically, my research focuses on the modeling, analysis and control of biological systems with main applications in crop protection: plant pest and disease epidemiology, design cost--effective and environmentally friendly control strategies. My main areas of expertise are ordinary differential equation models, which can be coupled to discrete equations, partial differential equations, impulsive differential equations, optimization and optimal control problems.

I supervise master’s and doctoral students and collaborates with teams in mathematics and agronomic sciences.
